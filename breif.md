# Breif
- Audience: People visiting this site should be interested in the outdoors, and looking for references to places they can visit. 
- Content: The content will be images and descriptions of me or my friends on our trips. There will be brief descriptions and in cases directions in order to get to the places the photos were taken. 
- Three Adjectives: Exciting, Simple, Intriguing
- Mood Board: https://www.chrisburkard.com/Pages/Portfolio, https://www.alexhonnold.com/, https://emilyharrington.com/
- Palette: Cream/Egg White (#FCF9EA), Dark/Deep Evergreen(#354024), Yellow (#D6A42F)
- Signature Element: In depth descriptions and directions to locations.
