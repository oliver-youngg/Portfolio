Baxter State Park, Maine · Hiking

Katahdin

04 PHOTOS
Location & directions ＋

Where: Baxter State Park, Maine.

Getting there: Enter through the park’s Togue Pond Gate and follow the Park Tote Road to Roaring Brook Campground for the Chimney Pond trail. The drive from the gate takes about 30 minutes. Katahdin has several trailheads, so choose the route that matches your hike.

Day hikers using a Katahdin trailhead need a Katahdin Trailhead Pass; check Baxter State Park’s current access information before you go.

Maine · On the water

Kennebec River

04 PHOTOS

River days on the Kennebec in Maine.

Location & directions ＋

Where: Kennebec River near The Forks, Maine.

Getting there: From I-95, take Exit 133 at Fairfield and follow ME-201 north through Skowhegan and Bingham to The Forks. The common whitewater reach starts at Harris Station; the put-in and take-out depend on the run and current access arrangements. Confirm the launch, shuttle, and river conditions with your outfitter or access provider before setting out.

The Forks / Harris Station access directions (outfitter information).

Bristol, New Hampshire · Whitewater kayaking

Ayers Island Dam

02 PHOTOS

Whitewater kayaking at Ayers Island Dam in Bristol, New Hampshire.

Location & directions ＋

Where: Pemigewasset River at Ayers Island Dam, Bristol, New Hampshire.

Getting there: From NH Route 104, turn onto Ayers Island Road. Pass the ball field, follow the road around it, then turn left toward the dam-side put-in. Parking is limited near the put-in; arrange the take-out and shuttle before launching.

Ayers Island Dam reach and access notes.

New Hampshire · Hiking

Mount Washington

01 PHOTO
Location & directions ＋

Where: Mount Washington summit, New Hampshire.

Getting there: For the Auto Road, use 1 Mount Washington Auto Road, Gorham, NH 03581. The entrance is on NH Route 16 in Pinkham Notch, about 8 miles south of Gorham. The summit is also reached by the Cog Railway and hiking trails; choose and check the approach that fits your trip.

Auto Road directions · Mount Washington State Park visitor information.

Location & directions ＋

Where: Monadnock Mtn

Getting there: For Mount Monadnock, use 169 Poole Road, Jaffrey, NH 03452. From Jaffrey Center, take NH Route 124 west to Dublin Road, then turn onto Poole Road and continue to the park entrance, about 10 minutes from downtown Jaffrey. Parking is limited, especially on weekends and holidays, so reserving a day-use spot ahead of time is recommended. The summit is reached by hiking trails only, such as the White Dot and White Cross trails, and the park does not offer camping.

Monadnock State Park visitor information · Call 603-532-8862 for trail conditions.
